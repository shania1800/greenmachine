The Other Five

INTEL.GENTLE.SHADE

A completely objective, deeply respectful, scientifically rigorous breakdown of our beloved competition.

RED
Threat // Spicy
"Stop sign cosplayers"
Look, we get it. Red is the color of fire trucks, ketchup, and that one Power Ranger. Loud, hot, very emotional. We respect the energy. We just don't respect the strategy. See you at the finish line — we will leave the porch light on.
ORANGE
Threat // Mild
"Red, but make it scared"
Orange is what happens when red tries to commit but blinks. It is the color of traffic cones, Cheetos dust, and "we couldn't decide." Bless their hearts. They are going to have a wonderful day finishing in the middle.
YELLOW
Threat // Glare
"Walking high-vis vest"
We see you. Literally. From space. Yellow is the color of bananas, school buses, and caution tape — three things that go down faster than your team is about to. Bright doesn't mean fast. Sorry babes.
BLUE
Threat // Lukewarm
"Mood: literally"
Blue is for oceans, sad songs, and what your team is going to be feeling at 4:55 PM when the scores are read. The branding is clean, the energy is calm. Calm doesn't win Color Wars. We do.
PURPLE
Threat // Loud
"Drama club captains"
Purple talks the most. Purple posts the most. Purple has Opinions. Purple has Theatre. Purple is going to have a very dramatic time watching us win. We brought tissues. Royal-purple ones. You're welcome.
GREEN
SELF // FACTS
"Just built different"
Green is money. Green is go. Green is the color of every dollar bill and every traffic light that means get out of our way. The other five are picking outfits. We are picking up the trophy.
